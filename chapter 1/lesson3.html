Brief Forms

There are some words in the English language that are frequently used. To save time, we can write them in shorthand using brief forms. The brief forms are written in a single stroke. The following are some of the brief forms:


1. S, P, & B

S, P, & B are most frequently used vowels in the English language. The shorthand A and E represents with a circle stroke. They only differ in sizes. E is a smaller circle than A.



P is a very important sound in English language. The circle can be written in either directions depending on principles or the next strokes.

B is a very important sound in English language. The circle can be written in either directions depending on principles or the next strokes.
