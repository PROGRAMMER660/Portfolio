Why Gregg Shorthand?

Gregg Shorthand is a phonetic writing system[cite: 1] that enables users to write at the speed of speech. It replaces long words with smooth, continuous strokes based on natural handwriting motions.

Tips & Guidance

Recommended supplies, practice routines, and common mistakes to avoid in Gregg Shorthand.

Recommended Supplies

  • Pens: Fine-point fountain pens or smooth gel pens (0.5mm).
  • Notebooks: Steno notebooks with vertical center lines and green-tinted paper.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Drawing Strokes: Avoid slow, deliberate drawing. Shorthand relies on continuous cursive motion.
  • Incorrect Proportions: Failing to distinguish between small (S), medium (F), and large (V) curves.
